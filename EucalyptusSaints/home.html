~ The Story ~

Oh man, the first time I saw Eucalyptus Saints in Tbilisi is something I'll never forget! These guys came out of nowhere around 2001, blending Tropicalia and Punk like it was the most natural thing in the world, and they tore up the local scene. First, there’s Luka "The Hurricane" Arveladze on lead vocals. This guy is pure energy on stage, always wearing those flamboyant shirts that make him look like he’s at a beach party, even in the middle of winter. His voice can make you want to dance and start a mosh pit at the same time. Then there's Nino "Strings" Ioseliani on the bass guitar. Nino’s super chill offstage but transforms into a total powerhouse when she picks up that bass. You can feel every groove in your bones when she plays. Giorgi "Gio" Abashidze handles the drums with serious charisma. I once saw him break two drumsticks during a show and didn’t miss a beat—literally. The dude’s a machine and the heartbeat of their confrontational style. Finally, there’s Tamari Kinkladze on synthesizer. She adds that tropical twist to the band's punk vibe with keys that can make you sway as much as headbang. Tamari's always got a smile and seems to love tossing in the unexpected sound that makes everyone cheer. Seeing them live is like being caught up in a storm of sound and fun. If you ever get the chance to catch the Eucalyptus Saints, do it! You'll walk out with your ears ringing and a grin on your face.
