Backstory | Band Photo | Members | Discography | Guestbook

~ The Story ~

Hey, let me tell you about this incredible band I saw live back in the day called Midnight Parlor. They came together in Lagos in 1974 and had this killer Afro-Radiant Rhythms vibe, mixing Go-Go and Soul in a way you wouldn't believe! First up, there's Kofi "Groove" Mensah on the bass guitar. Kofi was the backbone of the band, man. His energy was infectious. Always smiling, always keeping the groove tight. He made you feel like you were part of something bigger just by watching him play. Then there was Bisi "Soulfire" Adeyemi on the lead vocals. Her voice... oh, it was something else! She could light up the room—warm and electric at the same time. She had this way of captivating the audience with her charisma and soulful spirit. People said she could sing the phone book and make it sound like a hit. On the drums, they had Tunde "Tick-Tock" Ogunleye. He was the heartbeat of the group. Always had a joke at the ready and somehow managed to keep perfect time while making everyone laugh. You could just tell he loved what he did. And finally, Emeka "Keys" Okafor on the synthesizer. Emeka was a bit of a mystery—quiet but always thinking ahead. He'd come up with these wild sounds that seemed to take you on a journey. Somehow, he tied everything together. They met through the local music scene, jamming in basements and bars, and it was magic from the start. Seeing them live was like witnessing pure joy and talent come together. Midnight Parlor wasn't just a band; they were an experience.
